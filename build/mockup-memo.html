1Purpose and scope

This memo records how the AI features operated by Acme Analytics SAS meet the transparency duties of Article 50 of the EU AI Act, in force since 2 August 2026. It covers the AI systems that interact with end users or generate synthetic content in the course of Acme's B2B analytics product.

It is written to be attached to procurement and security replies. It states facts about the product as declared by Acme during intake; it is not legal advice and does not replace a conformity assessment.

BQuestionnaire answer pack (extract)

Q3 of 12
Does your product use AI systems that interact with natural persons?
Yes. The Acme Assistant answers user questions in-app. Users are informed of this on first use and by a persistent label (snippet CS-01). See hosted page Disclosures section and register entry RS-01.
Q4 of 12
Are AI-generated outputs marked in a machine-readable way?
Yes. Every exported report and chart carries a C2PA manifest and XMP metadata recording synthetic origin (register entry RS-02). A visible “AI-assisted” label is also shown.
Q7 of 12
Do you operate emotion-recognition or biometric categorisation?
No. The sentiment panel was retired in August 2026 and is not deployed for any EU customer. Declared as not in use in the register (entry RS-03).
