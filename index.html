Questions frequently asked

Am I really in scope of Article 50?

If your product has a chatbot or AI assistant that interacts with users, generates synthetic media, or uses emotion recognition, and you sell into the EU — yes. Article 50 has applied since 2 August 2026. The only grace period (2 December 2026) covers machine-readable marking for generative systems already on the market, and only on the provider side. The trigger in practice isn't the regulator: it's buyers sending the questionnaire before signing.

Is this legal advice?

No, and we don't pretend otherwise. TrustPage turns your statements about your product into documentary evidence: a memo, a register, disclosures and answers. You stay responsible for the facts — the pack is what you show a buyer, not an opinion you wave at a regulator.

Where does the page live?

On a hosted subdomain (yourbrand.trustpage.ai) — nothing to deploy, TLS included, link-ready for every security reply. The free plan carries a discreet "generated with TrustPage" mark; paid plans don't.

We have no compliance person on the team.

That's exactly who this is for. Most EU-facing SaaS are 1–50 people; the questionnaire doesn't care. The pack is written in plain language and takes about 2 hours of your time, once.

What about the December marking deadline?

The pack includes machine-readable marking guidance for generative output (Art. 50(2)) and the disclosure snippets to go in your product today — chatbot label, voice disclosure, AI-text label — so the visible side of compliance isn't a redesign project.
